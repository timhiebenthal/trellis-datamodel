# Documenting Business Processes with trellis

`**Disclaimer:**` Experimental Feature.  
Therefore it needs to be activated explicitly through the `trellis.yml` or in the UI as shown in the clip below. 

## Create Events and annotate them

Capture business events in trellis and derive their dimensions using the 7W's of the BEAM* Modeling Approach.
Leverage Role-Playing Dimensions to have a single table covering several processes.

## Documenting Entity Lineage

Combine multiple events into a process, consolidating the dimensions and facts into a single star-schema.
You then also can view a filtered canvas for this particular process or event, ideal for larger, clutterered projects
